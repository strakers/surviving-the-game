---
title: Floor Lords
---

Floor lords are special monsters of great prowress found on each floor fo the labyrinth. They require special conditions
to be summoned and are usually only intentionally summoned by large clans or expedition teams due to their immense power
and danger level.

Below are the floor lords known to explorers.

## Floor 1

- Dreadfear, Lord of Terror
- Berzac, Lord of the Abyss
