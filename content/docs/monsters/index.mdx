Monsters are adversarial creatures in the world of Dungeon & Stone, which, upon death, often provide valuable resources.

When monsters die in the [labyrinth](/docs/places/labyrinth), its body scatters into bright glowing dust and scatters,
sometimes leaving behind [items](/docs/items), [mana stones](/docs/concepts/mana-stones), or an [essence](/docs/essences).
These resources make monsters attractive for explorers to hunt, and, aside from the [experience](/docs/concepts/experience)
gained from defeating a monster, are the prime inspiration for labyrinth exploration.
Additionally, with the support of a mage casting distortion magic, a monster's body can be preserved for the purpose of
extracting monster materials, often used for crafting, or [other culteral uses](/docs/groups/races/barbarians#engravings).

<Spoiler minChapter={500}>
  One exception to this is in the Basement level of the labyrinth, where some of the monsters did not dissipate when
  defeated and their bodies remained.[^ch500]
</Spoiler>

Monsters occupy each floor of the labyrinth, with the weaker, or lower-classed monsters generally occupying the lower
floors. Monsters are ranked according to difficulty, with a lower number rank indicating a higher level of difficulty -
similar to explorers.

## Classification

Aside from normal monsters, there are several additional classes of monsters known to explorers.

### Mutants

Variants of monsters which posses a class different from the original - for example, a goblin archer is a goblin mutant.

### High-ranking Species

Monsters whose rank is higher than the average floor rank.

### Rare Species

Monsters who can only be found in rifts, or are extremely difficult to encounter are known as rare species. One example
of these are the [rats] on the 4th floor.

### High-ranking Variant

These monsters are special monsters which posses their own name and higher level of intelligence. They are much more
dangerous and powerful than the normal forms, and possess a much higher rate of dropping an essence or [numbered item*](/docs/items).
Additionally, defeating one such monster will always grant an additional experience point. Lastly, the essences of this
type are much stronger and often contain multiple [active abilties](/docs/abilities#active). These monsters are known
to [players](/docs/concepts/evil-spirits) as "Boss" monsters.
