## Arcs

### Early Progression Arcs (Chapters 1–100)

- Tutorial Arc (Chapters 1–3): Hansu Lee transmigrates into the body of Bjorn Yandel and completes the game's initial tutorial.
- Goblin Arc (Chapters 4–15): Bjorn navigates the early floors of the dungeon, focusing on survival and initial resource gathering.
- Return in Rafdonia Arc (Chapters 16–22): His first official return to the city and the beginning of his life as a resident barbarian.
Bloody Citadel Arc (Chapters 23–36): Challenges within the Bloody Citadel and the introduction of initial guild conflicts.
- Free Barbarian Arc (Chapters 37–48): Bjorn's independence and the forging of new alliances while navigating city politics.
- Little Balkan Arc (Chapters 49–71): Includes the "Team Half-Wit" formation and the significant "Witch's Forest" events.
- Knight Crusher Arc (Chapters 72–100): Focuses on the sewerage investigation, the Baron Martoin incident, and rising rumors about Bjorn's identity. 

### Mid-Stage Arcs (Chapters 101–300) 
- Glacier Cave Arc (Chapters 101–118): Exploration of higher-tier floors and major confrontations with PKers (Player Killers).
- Old Hand Arc (Chapters 119–130): Introduction of Baekho Lee and the "Watcher of the Round Table".
- Labyrinth of Larcaz Arc (Chapters 131–146): A large-scale expedition involving team play and the "Orcules" encounter.
- Apple Narak Arc (Chapters 147–160): A shorter, intense arc focusing on the specific challenges of the Apple Narak region.
- Doppelganger Forest Arc (Chapters 161–182): Centered on identity and psychological challenges within the Doppelganger Forest.
- Bifron Arc (Chapters 191–208): High-stakes encounters involving powerful entities and further world-building.
- First Floor & Noark Arc (Chapters 209–240): Significant developments regarding the underground city of Noark.
- Baronet Arc (Chapters 241–260): Bjorn’s rising social status and nobility-related responsibilities.
- Farune Island Arc (Chapters 261–300): A major expedition to the island, introducing new high-rank monsters and rewards. 

### Advanced Arcs (Chapters 301–500)

- Iron Mask Arc (Chapters 301–350): Deepening mysteries surrounding other players and the "Iron Mask" figure.
- Riehen Schuitz Arc (Chapters 351–420): Major political and combat developments involving the Riehen Schuitz faction.
- Holy Land/Relic Arcs (Chapters 421–480): Following the events of the Riehen Schuitz Arc, Bjorn interacts with the game's foundational lore, focusing on sacred relics and the acient history of the Raphdonia.
- The Second Coming of Noark (Chapters 481–530): Intensifying conflict with the underground city of Noark and its terrorist actions against the Crown.
- Exploration of the 7th–8th Floors (Chapters 531–600): Transitioning into high-rank dungeon exploration, including massive boss encounters and rare item collection. 
- Bjorn begins his real estate and apartment construction project in District 7, while avoid the prospect of marriage.
- 

The District 7 Reconstruction (Chapters 550–620):
Bjorn utilizes his status to take over the development of District 7.
He introduces the concept of modern apartments to maximize housing for explorers.
This is the "Business Mogul" phase where he is balancing city politics with dungeon crawling.
The Ragna Marriage Proposal (Chapters 630–650):
This happens during his rise in noble circles.
He refuses the proposal due to the "Doll" (the soul/vessel issue) and his unique circumstances as an "Evil Spirit."
The Tower Master & Outside the Walls (Chapters 660–700+):
Bjorn goes outside the city walls with the Tower Master.
They end up trapped/isolated in the "outside world" alongside Baek Ho's team.
This arc is famous for shifting the scale of the world beyond the Labyrinth and Rafdonia

### Advanced Progression Arcs (Chapters 600–800) 

- The Fallen King Arc (Chapters 601–680): Large-scale world-building reveals regarding the "Immortal King" and the origins of the Labyrinth.
- The Evil Spirit Possession Arc (Chapters 681–750): A critical arc dealing with the consequences of "Evil Spirits" (players) inhabiting the world and the tragic backstories of those lost to them.
- Crown & Conspiracy Arc (Chapters 751–800): Political maneuvering within Rafdonia's royalty, Bjorn's rising status to high nobility (Viscount), and the preparation for the final layers of the dungeon.
