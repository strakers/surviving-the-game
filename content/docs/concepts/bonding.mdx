---
title: Bonding Magic
---

Bonding magic binds explorers together for the next 24 hours, allowing them to travel together in the labyrinth and be located together through portal travel. 

The bonding spell must be performed by a mage, and a charge is often associated when using a non-affiliate mage. This cost increases by rank, which is "measured by the total amount of essence engraved on the soul". [^ch22]

Initially the bonding magic can bind a maximum of 5 individuals.

Later, this increases to 6 people.

The cost to bind 2 rank-9 explorers is 15,000 stones.
