Magic is the mystical arts used by mages. Through the use of mana, many special effects and feats are possible using spells. Mages need to study to use magic, and many spend their lifetines dedicated to furthering their knowledge and craft.

## Mages

"I’m realizing the status that mages hold. Powerful attack magic. Highly versatile support abilities like the Wound Aggravation Curse. And the knowledge they’ve accumulated by reading numerous books." - Bjorn [^ch30]

Mages are necessary for gathering monster materials and storing essences. Using magic, essences can be stored in test tubes and used later. [^ch31]
