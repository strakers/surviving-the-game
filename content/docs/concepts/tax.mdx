---
title: Taxes
---

In the city of [Raphdonia], (almost) all citizens must pay tax to the royal family. Failure to pay taxes results in
execution. This is the harsh reality [players] find themselves in and must navigate. Many individuals choose the high-risk,
high-reward nature of [labyrinth] exploration to gather funds to pay taxes, while other seek safer means, such as
employment within the city.

Those who are unable to pay their taxes by a given date are sentenced to execution by beheadding. 

There's a superstition that drinking the blood of those executed by the tax collector brings financial luck. Wome townsfolk dip bread into the blood of the beheaded and eat it in hipes of attaining this luck. [^ch18]

The first year tax is 700,000 stones, with an increat of approx 100,000 syones per year to a certain limit (unknown). [ch^18]
