## Magical Types

### mage
casts spells. cannot use essence and do not use soul power

### magician
differs from a mage in that they gain abilities through essence, rather than knowledge and study

#### healer

#### sorcerer

#### shaman

## Divine Types

### paladin
combine divine spells and weaponry. cannot use essence, need to pray for divine blessings

### priest
person of the cloth, aligned with one of the following: reatlas, tovera, heindal, karui
