---
title: Evil Spirit
---

A player from our world that completes the game and is transported to Raphdonia. They take over the body of a resident at the age of 20, of which the original mind/soul is lost.

Evil spirits are labelled as personsa non grata and are flagged for execution once discovered.

The events of Dungeon and Stone take place the day after King Ravigion III dies, which is 150 years ago, according to the year Bjorn arrived. [ch^19]
