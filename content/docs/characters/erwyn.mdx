Erwin is an elf of noble blood

<InfoCard />



Introduced to her early in the story as she roams the labyrinth alone. She becones Bjorn's "[night companion]", as they spend a night in the labyrinth watching over each other.

Bjorn later learns that she is being pursued by a clan after one of their members attempted to s-assault her (and in defense, she cut off his member). he decides to help her and form a temporary partnership. [^ch10]
