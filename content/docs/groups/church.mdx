The church is the sole religious organization within [Raphdonia](/docs/places/raphdonia). It's current state of worship observes 3 deities:

- Tobera, god of the sun
- Realtlas, goddess of the stars
- Heindel, god of the moon

## Worship

Followers usually worship one of the 3 deities, though there is no stated restriction on revering multiple deities.

Members of the church follow dedication to one of the deities, and receive blessings from them.

## Structure

The church is led by a pope, followed by archbishops, bishops, priests, and paladins. Priests and priestesses provide healing, relief, and support to the local community, while paladins provide security, enforcement, and interrogations (when necessary). There are also saints which can receive oracles from their respective deity, though these revelations place a heavy burden on their bodies.

## Divisions

### Tobera

Due to an event long ago, priests and priestesses are told not to provide hwaling to Barbarians.

### Realtlas

Also known as the goddess of destiny, she has provided support to [Bjorn](/docs/characters/bjorn) on hi journey several timea.

### Heindal

Not much has been revealed about this deity, though we have met an affiliated priestess.

### Karui

Karui is a dark god not associated with the church, but members of the church can become corrupted by his power and become a karui priest.
