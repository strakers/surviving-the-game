The royal army is the military force of [Raphdonia]() and is divided into several divisions:

  - Royal Knights
  - Mage Corps
  - Strategic Department
  - Investigation Department
  - Ministry of Information
  - Secret Police
  - Rose Knights

## Royal Knights

The royal knights are the primary branch of the Royal Army, divided further into smaller companies:

- The 1st company, led by the Knighr of Light, [Jerome Saintred]()
- The 2nd company, led by the moon knight

## Mage Corps

The mage corps are a division comprised entirely of mages, for the purpose of support magic, bombardment, investigation, or long range communications. This division privides much utility to the army.

Led by sir [Kyle Pevrosk](/docs/characters/kyle-pevrosk), with [Alluva Raven](/docs/characters/raven) as his deputy commander.

## Rose Knights

The Rose Knights are a secret branch of the army reporting directly to the King and Chancellor. This small force of warrior women are a powerful team of ruby red aura wielding assassins, mobilized either for covert missions or to efficiently eliminate high profile targets.
