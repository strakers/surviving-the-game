title: Barbarians
---

Barbarians are one of the six races found in (Raphdonia)[]. They are generally characterized as goants, with large builds, and increased strength and durability. Barbarians are thought to have lower intelligence, though this is a misunderstanding of their cultural habits and social interactions.

Barbarians are able to receive emgravings to enhance their abilities and prowress.
