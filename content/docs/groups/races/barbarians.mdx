---
title: Barbarians
---


Barbarians are one of the six races available for play within Dungeon & Stone, as well as found within the city of [Raphdonia](/docs/places/raphdonia). They are generally characterized as giants, with large builds, and increased strength and durability. Barbarians are thought to have lower intelligence, though this is a misunderstanding of their cultural habits and social interactions.

Barbarians are able to receive emgravings to enhance their abilities and prowress.

Their sanctuary is located at the edge of [District 7](/docs/places/ravigion#district-7)
