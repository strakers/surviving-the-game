---
title: Fairies
---

Fairies are one of the six races available for play within Dungeon & Stone, as well as found within the city of [Raphdonia](/docs/places/raphdonia).
They are generally characterized as slender, with pointed ears, and enhanced senses. Fairies are attuned to the spirits and are capable of creating "bonds" or contracts with elemental spirits in order to enhance their abilities.

A Spirit Stone is an item that allows fairies to make contracts with spirits of other attributes. In the game, the price of a Spirit Stone was about 150,000 stones, and the success rate was only 10%. [^ch19]
