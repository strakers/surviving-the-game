---
title: Dwarves
---

Dwarves are one of the six races available for play within Dungeon & Stone, as well as found within the city of [Raphdonia](/docs/places/raphdonia). They are generally known for their short stature, as well as for their engineering and craftsmanship. Many operate as blacksmiths or some other form of builder, and they possess a racial trait to repair equipment.
