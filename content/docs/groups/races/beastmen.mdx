Beastmen are one of the six races available for play within Dungeon & Stone, as well as found within the city of [Raphdonia](/docs/places/raphdonia).
They are known to resemble animals and have close bonds with them. They can also contract with a spirit animal in order to bosltster their abilities.

Their sanctuary is located within [District 13](/docs/places/ravigion#district-13),

## Tribes

Beastmen are divided into several tribes - we've been introduced to the following:

- **Red Cat tribe**, of which Misha belongs to.
- **Black Bear tribe**, of which Abman belongs to.
- **Blue Wolf tribe**, ...
- **White Rabbit tribe**, of which Baron Ledadasdadadada belongs to.
