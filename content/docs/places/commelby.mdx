The middle region within the city of [Raphdonia], comprising of Districts 2 - 5, and also housing the city's free market.

"That’s what they call the 2nd to 5th districts that surround the 1st district, the Imperial City of Karnon, in a ring shape. Many people simply call it the free market. It’s the most commercially active place in the city. Lafdonia’s middle class resides here, and many artisans and shops are concentrated in this area." [^ch43]
