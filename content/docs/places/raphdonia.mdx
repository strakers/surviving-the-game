Raphdonia is the main setting of the game Dungeon & Stone, as well as the city in which players are transported after completing the game.

## Regions

The city is broken up into smaller regions and districts:

- [Karnon](./karnon) - the city's capitol
- [Kommelby](./kommelby) - the location of the free market
- [Gnome Tree](./gnome-tree) - agricultural and resort region
- [Ravigion](./ravigion.mdx) - general residential area
- [Bifron](./bifron) - the city's main prison

There is also a forbidden sub-region deep underneath the city:
- [Noark](./noark) - a refuge for outcasts from the city
