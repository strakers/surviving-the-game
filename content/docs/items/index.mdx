Items are tools and resources available for use in the game of [Dungeon & Stone] as well as the world of [Raphdonia].

Items can be equipped by any person (transferable), bound to a single person - usually until death (bound), or consumed when used (consumable). There are also items which modify status/states when worn/used (modifiers); and those that trigger effects, events, or abilities (triggerables).

## Categories

Items can be generally organized into the followibg categories:

- wearables
- consumables
- triggerables

### Wearables

As the name suggests, wearables are items that can be worn on a person. These include equipment such as clothes or armor, talismans which provide permanent or reusable buffs or effects, or utility items such as storage space rings or bags.

### Consumables

Consumables are items which provide single-use effects on the user (and sometimes a party or area) and disappear. These vary in type, from potions and cures to restore resources or remove curses/statis ailments, items which provide permanent or temporary stat increases, and those which provide helpful wffects, like warding away monsters or providing light.

### Triggerables

These are similar to the last category of consumables, only many of these can be multi-use, have unlimited reusability, or degrade after use and must be restored. These include items such as rift stones which open rifts, scrolls which activate stored magic, or trap items.
