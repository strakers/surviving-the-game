Notes on things I've noticed throughout the story.

- Opening a portal for the first time grants EXP+2, but also increases the maximum amoubt of absorbable essences. This is huge in the later stages, where each essence exponentially increases combat ability. This bonus is only granted to the first group during a labyrinth run, as portals either stay open or only open once for the duration of the labyrinth run. [^ch22] For this reason, some explorers attempt speed-runs.

- magic torch, its a magic item about a hand's span, and once lit, last for 3 days. [^ch23]
- soul power increases by 10 every level up to level 5 [^ch202]
- dragon blessings: earth dragon increases essence stats by 20%, volcani  dragon increases skill performance by 30%, sea dragon increases soul power by 100 [^ch202]
- after a rift has opened, it usually takes some time before they can be opened/spawned again. on the first floor, it takes at least 3 months, though its usually 5 or 6, and never more than 8.  [^ch27] Once opened, thousands of portals to the same place open, though the rift only accepts the first few that enter. Many portals accept max 5 people but some max 3 or even 1. [^ch28]
- Mages must prove their skills before being allowed in the labyrinth. This is because they are a valuable resource to the city of [Raphdonia]. [^ch28]
- It wad the mages who first discovered the existence of evil spirits. [^ch28]
